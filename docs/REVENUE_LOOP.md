# Revenue loop

The engine is designed around verified economics rather than promises.

1. Discover current demand signals.
2. Capture source, timestamp, claim and confidence.
3. Score each opportunity on demand, margin, speed, cost, competition, risk and defensibility.
4. Design the smallest sellable offer.
5. Run a bounded acquisition experiment.
6. Record sales, costs, refunds and retention.
7. Compare observed economics with estimates.
8. Keep, iterate or kill the experiment.
9. Feed measured outcomes back into future prioritization.

Financial side effects remain behind the risk gateway. Research mode cannot execute financial actions; live actions can be capped and routed for confirmation.
